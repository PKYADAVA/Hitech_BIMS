{% comment %} What the sheet comes to, under the columns it totals rather than in a block of its own: a figure headed "Total Amount" three inches from the Amount column is a figure somebody has to match up by eye. Two of these are averages and not sums. Avg Wt. is the sheet's whole weight over its whole bird count, and Rate is its whole value over its whole weight — weighted, because adding a column of per-kg rates together produces a number that means nothing, and averaging them unweighted lets a 40-bird line pull the figure as hard as a 4,000-bird one. {% endcomment %}

{% if instance %} {% comment %} What the phone captured on site: the truck, the birds, the weighbridge slip — the pictures that make a slip nobody at the desk witnessed checkable. The pictures were read-only here, on the reasoning that evidence the desk can alter is not evidence. What that overlooked is the ordinary case: a photo of the wrong lorry, a thumb over the lens, a slip photographed twice and the real one missed. There was no way to correct any of it from the desk, and the register's only remedy was deleting the whole sale. So a picture can be dropped and another put up — every change through the same scoped API the phone posts to, and never for somebody whose edits go to approval, whose photo change would otherwise skip the review their figures are waiting on. The card is shown on every sale being edited now, not only the ones that already carry evidence: a sale raised at the desk is exactly the one somebody may want to attach the weighbridge slip to afterwards. {% endcomment %}
