Every phase of a flock's programme is listed whatever its age, tagged currentdoneupcoming in the Phase column — an upcoming phase has nothing due yet but carries a Total Required Feed to procure against. Scheduled to Date (cumulative intake) is feed due by today — the breed standard's intake accumulated across each phase's age band, where Day's Intake is that same curve read for today alone. pro marks a figure pro-rated from the phase cap where the breed has no standard curve, cap the whole cap on an open-ended final phase. Difference is that cumulative figure less Consumed — positive means the flock is behind its curve, negative means ahead of it. Feed Outlook is built on what the flock is actually eating — the mean of its last three entries for that feed. A std on Daily Rate, Days Cover or Next 3 Days means there were no recent entries to average and the figure is projected from the breed standard's daily intake instead. Action Required reads red for today, amber for this week.
